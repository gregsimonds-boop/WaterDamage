Rain, humidity and coastal weather are part of Miami-Dade life. Understanding the restoration process can help you ask useful questions and choose your next steps.

01

Ask about availability

Confirm service hours, your ZIP code and the earliest available appointment with the provider. Availability is not guaranteed.

02

Explain what happened

Describe the source of the water, affected rooms and when you noticed the damage.

03

Made for South Florida

Ask about experience with Miami-Dade properties, moisture and storm-related damage.

04

Clear, careful communication

Request a written scope, pricing, documentation and an explanation of what happens next.
